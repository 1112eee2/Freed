- Threefold repetition is supported during play but is history-dependent; reusable exact tablebases currently prove outcomes under the explicit no-progress rule with repetition disabled. Adding automatic threefold can turn a tablebase win into an earlier draw, but cannot turn its non-loss strategy into a loss.
- The six-piece frontier has passed the project's current exhaustive threshold/prefix acceptance regimen, but this is an endgame-frontier result rather than a proof of the 8-vs-8 start. No larger frontier is currently a completion requirement; it should be considered only if accepted six-piece-guided DFPN cannot close the root.
- The full initial-position proof certificate remains incomplete and the root remains `UNKNOWN` at proof/disproof 288,000/1,032,704 after 566,173,881 post-rebase expansions. `retarget-dfpn-solved` passed an automated checksum-validating fixture that retained only exact resolved records, atomically changed the root header, reloaded under the new root, and rejected the old root; it enables bounded root-move partitions without treating unresolved work as proof.
- The independent bitboard rules auditor is implemented and test-covered, but a final full audit from the initial position remains pending because the root strategy certificate does not exist yet.
- The version-1 headers have no reserved space for cryptographic provenance, so the committed `TABLEBASE_PROVENANCE.json` supplies the non-disruptive binding instead. It pins canonical `RULESET.json` SHA-256 `708bffcc2a264929616ba9cff8e63e926a96b992fced9692fe88feded2bd0e6a`, audited generator-source bundle SHA-256 `4dd86960222ffbb25ac90de7f1e2a69fd20ea36fc58e07c907fe54063ffcfc14`, header FNV/metadata, sizes, and complete-file SHA-256 for the accepted 4/5/6-piece files. `verify-tablebase-provenance` streamed all 5.38 GB and passed; payload, ruleset, and generator-source tamper tests are automated.
